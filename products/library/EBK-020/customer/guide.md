# Quarterly review for a small firm

*Look at the last quarter's sales, delivery, and cash without a strategy offsite.*

## The numbers you need

Invoices issued, invoices paid, costs, and the work that slipped. If you cannot get those four, the review is a conversation about feelings. Get the numbers first.

- Issued.
- Paid.
- Costs.
- Slipped work.

## What repeated

Which offer sold, which client type paid late, and which job always overran. One example is a story. Three examples are a pattern.

- The offer that sold.
- The late payer pattern.
- The overrun.

## One change

Pick one change for the next quarter. A price, a scope boundary, or a client you will not take again. Write it as a sentence you can check in ninety days.

- One change.
- A sentence.
- A date to check.

## What you will not do

You will not rewrite the brand, hire three people, and launch a course in the same quarter because the review felt ambitious. Ambition without a number is a mood.

- Write the not-doing list.
- Share it with the person who would otherwise start the extra project.
- Look again in ninety days.
