# Annual money review

*FIN-020 · version 1.0.0*

Look back at twelve months of surplus and set a single figure for next year.

Each month has income, costs, and a surplus formula. The review shows the total, the average, the best month, and the worst month. Next year's monthly target is something you type, and the sheet shows the gap against the average.
