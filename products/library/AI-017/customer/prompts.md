# Onboarding email prompts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## First email

Use when: They just joined.

Write the first email. Include the steps I list and the support address I give. Do not welcome them for a page. Do not promise a call I did not offer.

> Check: Steps match my list.

## Stuck

Use when: They have not finished step two.

Write a short email that names step two and the likely blocker I described. One link.

> Check: One link only.

## Done

Use when: They finished.

Write the email that says what they can do next, from my list. Do not upsell a product I did not name.

> Check: No extra product.

## Subject lines

Use when: You need subjects.

Write six subject lines that state the action. No fake 're:'.

> Check: Each subject is an action.

## Plain text

Use when: The mail should work without images.

Rewrite the email as plain text. Keep the links visible.

> Check: Links are visible.

## Consent

Use when: This is not a newsletter.

Say whether this email is about the thing they just bought. If I am mixing in news, separate it and say it needs consent.

> Check: Marketing is not hidden in the receipt.
