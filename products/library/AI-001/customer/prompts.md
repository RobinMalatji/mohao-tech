# Customer interview synthesis

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## List observations

Use when: After three or more interviews.

I will paste anonymised notes. Separate what a person said from what I might infer. Do not add a quote that is not in the notes. Group repeated observations and name how many notes support each one.

> Check: Every quote appears in the notes you pasted.

## Contradictions

Use when: When two customers disagree.

List the disagreements. Do not resolve them. For each, say which note it came from and what decision would be premature.

> Check: No disagreement is smoothed into a fake consensus.

## Jobs to be done

Use when: When you need the job, not a persona poster.

From these notes, write the job the person was hiring the product to do, the struggling moment, and what they used instead. If the notes do not say it, write 'not in the notes'.

> Check: You can point each line back to a note.

## Follow-up questions

Use when: Before the next interview.

Write six questions that the current notes cannot answer. Do not write questions the notes already answered.

> Check: Each question names the gap.

## Recruiting screen

Use when: When choosing who to interview next.

Given this target decision, write a five-question screen that rejects people who have not done the job in the last six months. Do not ask for sensitive personal data.

> Check: The screen does not ask for identity numbers or health information.

## Decision memo

Use when: When the team wants a recommendation.

Draft a one-page memo: decision, evidence from the notes, what the notes do not prove, and the next interview that would change the decision. Do not recommend a feature the notes do not support.

> Check: The memo has a 'not proved' section.
