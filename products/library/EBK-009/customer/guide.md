# Bookkeeping for a creator

*A monthly record of income, costs, and what you still owe yourself.*

## Separate the money

Use a separate account. Transfer a wage to yourself on a date, instead of spending from the business account when you are tired. The wage can be small. The habit matters.

- Separate account.
- A transfer date.
- Keep the personal spending out.

## The three lists

Income that has arrived. Invoices that have not. Costs of making the work. A sale that is not in the bank is not income yet in this simple system.

- Paid income.
- Unpaid invoices.
- Costs with receipts.

## Monthly close

Match the bank export to the lists. The unexplained lines are the work. Write what they were while you remember. Future you will not.

- Export on the first.
- Mark unexplained lines.
- File the receipts.

## When to get help

Get help when you register for VAT, hire someone, or cannot explain the bank. This guide is the habit before that, not a replacement for an accountant.

- VAT is a different set of rules.
- Payroll is a different set of rules.
- Ask before you guess a deduction.
