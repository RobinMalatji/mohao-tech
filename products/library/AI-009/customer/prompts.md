# Training session prompts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## Outcome

Use when: You have a topic and a time.

Write the outcome as something the learner can do at the end. If my topic is vague, ask what they must be able to do. Do not invent an accreditation.

> Check: No certificate claim was added.

## Timed plan

Use when: You have 60 minutes.

Split the hour into explanation, a worked example using my material, and practice. Include the minutes.

> Check: The minutes add up.

## Exercise

Use when: They need to try it.

Write one exercise they can finish in the room with the materials I listed. Do not require a tool I did not mention.

> Check: The exercise uses my materials.

## Check

Use when: You need to know it landed.

Write three questions that test the outcome, not attendance.

> Check: A person who missed the point would fail them.

## Handout

Use when: You need one page.

Turn the plan into a one-page handout. No stock advice that was not in the plan.

> Check: The handout matches the plan.

## What to drop

Use when: You are over time.

Tell me what to cut first if the group is slow. Do not cut the practice before the extra explanation.

> Check: Practice stays.
