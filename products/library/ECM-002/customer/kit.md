## A collection is a decision

A collection answers 'which of these is for me?'. 'All products' is not a collection. The sample shop uses Starter, Workshop, and Archive.

- Starter: under R250 and usable today.
- Workshop: a kit with a live session.
- Archive: older editions, price reduced, labelled as older.
