# Run a paid workshop

*Plan, price, and deliver a half-day workshop to a small group.*

## The promise

Write what the person will make by the end. A half day that 'explores' a topic is a talk. A workshop produces a draft, a sheet, or a decision.

- A thing they leave with.
- The skill required to start.
- The number of people.

## The room

Eight to sixteen people is enough for a first paid session. More than that and you cannot see the work. Online or in a room, plan how they will show you the draft.

- A cap.
- A way to see the work.
- Breaks in the plan.

## Price and cancel

Price it so that eight people pay for your day, including preparation. Write the cancel date. If you do not reach the minimum, you cancel on that date and refund. Do not slide the date in secret.

- A minimum.
- A cancel date.
- A refund rule.

## After

Send the files the same day. Ask one question: what will you use first? Their answers are the next session, or the reason not to run one.

- Files the same day.
- One question.
- Write down the answers.
