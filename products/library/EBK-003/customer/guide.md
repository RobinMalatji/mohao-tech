# Client onboarding checklist

*Start a project with the files, the decision maker, and the definition of done.*

## Before kickoff

Do not start because the deposit 'is on the way'. Start when the deposit is visible and the signed scope is in the folder.

- Deposit received.
- Scope file named and dated.
- One person who can approve.

## The questions

Ask who approves, how fast they can reply, and which files already exist. A brand project without the logo files is not ready. Write the answers into the project note, not into chat.

- Approver and backup.
- Reply time they can keep.
- Files they will send, with a date.

## The first week

Send a one-page plan: what you will show, when, and what you need from them before that date. If they miss the date, the plan moves. Do not absorb the slip and still promise Friday.

- One review date.
- The file you need before it.
- What 'approved' means.

## Close the kickoff

End the first call by reading back the decision, the date, and the owner. Send that in writing the same day.

- Three lines.
- No new scope.
- A calendar hold.
