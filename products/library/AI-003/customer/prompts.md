# Support reply drafts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## Missing file

Use when: A buyer cannot find the download.

Draft a reply that asks for the order reference, explains where the library link is, and does not blame the buyer. Do not promise a new file until the order is confirmed.

> Check: The reply asks for the reference.

## Wrong file

Use when: The buyer expected a different format.

Draft a reply that restates the formats listed in the product and offers to check the order. Do not invent a format the product does not have.

> Check: Formats match the product page.

## Refund request

Use when: Someone wants money back.

Draft a reply that acknowledges the request and says a person will check the refund policy. Do not approve or refuse the refund.

> Check: No refund is granted by the draft.

## Angry tone

Use when: The message is heated.

Rewrite my reply so it is short and specific. Remove sarcasm. Keep the decision I already made.

> Check: The decision is unchanged.

## Known issue

Use when: You have a real bug.

Draft a reply that states the bug, the workaround I paste below, and that I will write again when it is fixed. Do not give a date I did not provide.

> Check: No date was invented.

## Not our product

Use when: The question is about something else.

Draft a reply that says we cannot see that account and points them back to the company they named. Do not guess their password process.

> Check: No security steps were invented.
