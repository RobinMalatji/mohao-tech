# Product photo shot list

*This is a working draft for a South African online shop. It is not legal advice. Have a lawyer read it before you publish it as your policy.*

## The four frames

Front on a plain background. A detail of the material. The object next to a hand or a coin so scale is honest. The flaw, if there is one, rather than hiding it and answering emails later.

- Front.
- Detail.
- Scale.
- Flaw or variation.
