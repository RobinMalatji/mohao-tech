# After-purchase emails

*This is a working draft for a South African online shop. It is not legal advice. Have a lawyer read it before you publish it as your policy.*

## Receipt

Order reference, what they bought, the amount, the currency, and where to get the file. Do not say the file is attached if it is a link.

## How to open

One email that names the software. For a CSV, say to open it in a spreadsheet. For an SVG, say to upload it, and say it is not a Canva link if it is not.
