# A simple referral ask

*Ask customers for a referral without a gimmick.*

## Who to ask

Ask someone who has finished a job and said it went well. Do not ask in the middle of a revision. Do not ask everyone. Ask the people you would be proud to clone.

- Job finished.
- They were pleased.
- You would like another client like them.

## The ask

'If you know another bakery that needs this, I would like an introduction. I will treat them as well as I treated you.' Name the kind of business. A vague 'anyone' gets no one.

- Name the kind of customer.
- Offer to make the introduction easy.
- Do not offer a bribe you cannot explain.

## Make it easy

Send a two-line description they can forward. Do not attach a ten-page deck. Tell them you will follow up yourself so they are not responsible for the sale.

- Two lines.
- They are not the salesperson.
- You do the follow-up.

## Thank them

When a referral arrives, thank the person whether or not it becomes work. Tell them the outcome if the new client agrees. Do not vanish.

- Thank them the same week.
- Share the outcome if you may.
- Keep a list so you notice who refers.
