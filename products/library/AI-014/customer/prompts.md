# Plain-language policy prompts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## Plain clause

Use when: You have one clause.

Rewrite this clause in plain language. Then list any meaning you might have changed. Do not add rights.

> Check: The change list is empty or you fix it.

## Who it applies to

Use when: The subject is unclear.

Say who the clause binds, using only the nouns in the clause. If it is unclear, say unclear.

> Check: You do not guess the party.

## What the person must do

Use when: You need the obligation.

List the obligations as actions. If a sentence is not an obligation, leave it out and say so.

> Check: Obligations are quoted.

## Defined terms

Use when: Words are capitalised.

List defined terms and ask me for any definition that is not in the paste.

> Check: Missing definitions stay missing.

## Questions for counsel

Use when: Before you publish.

Write five questions a lawyer should answer. Do not answer them yourself.

> Check: You send the questions.

## Heading

Use when: The clause needs a label.

Write a heading a customer can scan. Do not make it friendlier than the clause.

> Check: The heading is accurate.
