# Customer interview guide

*Run five interviews that ask about past behaviour, not opinions about your idea.*

## Who to talk to

Talk to people who have done the job in the last six months. Friends who like you will be kind. Kind is not evidence.

- A screen question about the last time.
- No pitching in the first interview.
- Five people, not fifty.

## The questions

Ask when they last hit the problem, what they did, what it cost, and what they tried before. Ask them to walk through the last time, not the usual time.

- Last time, not usually.
- What they paid or spent.
- What they tried instead.

## During the call

Write their words. Do not translate into your feature while they are talking. If you do not understand, ask them to say it again. Do not fill the silence with your idea.

- Notes in their words.
- Silence is allowed.
- No demo.

## After five

List what repeated and what contradicted. Do not average people into a persona. A contradiction is a reason to talk to a sixth person, not a reason to pick the answer you prefer.

- Repeats.
- Contradictions.
- One decision the notes support.
