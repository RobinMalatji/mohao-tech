# Landing page copy sheet

*Write a one-page offer: who it is for, what they get, and what it costs.*

## The order of the page

Start with the customer and the result. Then what is included. Then the price. Then how they get it. A story about your journey can wait until someone has asked.

- Result in the first screen.
- Included, as a list.
- Price visible.

## Proof

Use a proof you have: a number of customers, a named client who agreed, or a sample of the file. Do not invent a review. If you have no proof yet, show the work.

- No invented reviews.
- Show a page of the product.
- Name the format.

## Objections

Answer the two questions buyers actually ask: what if it does not fit, and how do I get the file. Link to the refund policy you really have, not a policy you wish you had.

- Formats.
- The real refund rule.
- A support address.

## Cut

Read the page aloud. Delete a sentence that could sit on any other product. If the page still makes sense, it was too generic to keep.

- Delete one generic sentence.
- Read it aloud.
- Ask someone who is the customer.
