# Incident notes prompts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## Timeline

Use when: You have chat fragments.

Build a timeline with the times I wrote. Do not fill gaps with guesses. Mark gaps.

> Check: Gaps are marked.

## Impact

Use when: You need the effect.

Describe who was affected using only my notes. Do not estimate a number I did not give.

> Check: No invented count.

## What we know

Use when: Facts and theories are mixed.

Split the notes into facts and theories.

> Check: Theories are labelled.

## Follow-up

Use when: You need actions.

List follow-ups that my notes already imply. Do not assign a person I did not name.

> Check: Owners are blank when unknown.

## Customer note

Use when: People were affected.

Draft a short note that says what failed, what we did, and what we still do not know. No blame of a named person.

> Check: Unknowns stay in.

## Next time

Use when: You want one change.

Propose one change that would have made this incident easier to see. Label it a proposal.

> Check: It is not written as if it already happened.
