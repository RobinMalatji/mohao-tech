# Meeting summary prompts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## Decisions only

Use when: The notes are messy.

From these notes, list decisions. If a point was discussed but not decided, put it under 'not decided'. Do not add decisions.

> Check: Not-decided is present when needed.

## Owners

Use when: People volunteered.

Assign owners only where the notes name a person. Otherwise write 'owner not named'.

> Check: No owner was guessed.

## Short send

Use when: You need the email.

Write the follow-up email in under 150 words using only the decisions and owners list. No new topics.

> Check: The email matches the list.

## Risks mentioned

Use when: Someone raised a worry.

List worries that were said in the meeting. Do not solve them.

> Check: Each worry is from the notes.

## Agenda for next time

Use when: The meeting ran out.

Turn 'not decided' items into an agenda of five items or fewer.

> Check: The agenda does not add new work.

## Absent person

Use when: Someone missed it.

Write a five-line brief for the person who was absent. No jokes about them.

> Check: The brief is usable without the transcript.
