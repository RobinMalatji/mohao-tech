# Welcome email sequence

*Three emails after someone joins a list or buys a small product.*

## Email one

Send the thing they asked for, and one next step. If they bought a file, the next step is how to open it. If they joined a list, the next step is the guide you promised. Do not add a second offer.

- The promised thing.
- One next step.
- A human reply address.

## Email two

A few days later, answer the question people ask after they start. For a budget workbook, that question is which number to replace first. Teach that. Do not announce a new product.

- One question.
- A short answer.
- No new offer.

## Email three

Ask if it was useful, and tell them how to reply. A reply is more valuable than a click on a slogan. If you want to sell something else, that is a different email with its own consent.

- Ask one question.
- Separate any sales mail.
- Honour unsubscribe.

## What not to automate yet

Do not build a twelve-email machine before ten people have read email one. Write the three, send them, and change the sentence people did not understand.

- Ten real readers.
- Edit the confusing sentence.
- Then consider a fourth email.
