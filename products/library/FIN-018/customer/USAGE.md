# How to use Healthcare cost tracker

*Excel workbook with PDF guide*

The workbook annualises monthly premiums and adds invoices you pay yourself. It does not compare schemes and it does not tell you what to claim.

## Who it is for

Households who want one total for healthcare cash costs.

## Compatibility

- Microsoft Excel for Microsoft 365 or Excel 2021 and later
- Formulas also open in Google Sheets; confirm PMT, FV, and NPER after import
- PDF guide opens in any PDF reader
- DOCX guide opens in Microsoft Word or Google Docs

## Before you start

1. Read the README and this usage guide.
2. Open the main deliverable and replace every sample figure or sample sentence with your own.
3. Keep a copy of the original download before you edit it.
4. Check the licence before you share the edited file with anyone else.

## What you should see

- See premiums and invoices in one year
- Keep pharmacy, dental, and optical separate
- Avoid treating a premium as the whole cost
