# Healthcare cost tracker

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

The sample pays R3,200 a month for medical aid and R450 for gap cover, plus R2,360 of invoices. The year is R46,160. This is not a comparison of medical schemes and it is not medical advice.

## How to use the workbook

1. Enter the contribution that leaves your bank, including any dependants on the same debit.
2. Enter only the portion of an invoice you paid. Do not enter the full provider bill if the scheme paid part of it.
3. Add rows for other invoices and extend the SUM if you pass row 4.
4. Use the year total in your household budget as a reality check, not as a tax deduction.

## Worked example

Medical aid premiums are R38,400.

- Gap cover premiums are R5,400.
- Invoices are R2,360.
- The cash total is R46,160.

## A practical routine

- Update invoices in the month you pay them.
- Keep scheme certificates for tax season in the document checklist, not in this total.
- Ask the scheme or a broker about benefits. This sheet does not know them.

> This workbook organises figures you type in. It is not financial, credit, insurance, or tax advice. Rates, fees, and laws change. A qualified adviser should review decisions that affect debt, tax, medical cover, or retirement.
