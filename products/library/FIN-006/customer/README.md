# Four-week grocery tracker

*FIN-006 · version 1.0.0*

Price a repeating grocery list and see the cost per person across four weeks.

Enter the aisle, the usual quantity, and the price you last paid. The workbook extends one shop across four weeks and divides the month by the number of people eating.
