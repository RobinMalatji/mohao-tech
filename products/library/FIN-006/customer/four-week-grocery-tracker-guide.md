# Four-week grocery tracker

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

This is a price list, not a meal plan and not nutrition advice. The sample shop feeds three people and repeats four times. Bread, milk, and tins are counted in the quantities shown.

## How to use the workbook

1. Replace unit prices with the price on your last till slip.
2. Change quantities before you change the month formulas.
3. Set People eating to the number who regularly eat from this shop.
4. Use Four weeks as the grocery line in the household budget.

## Worked example

Maize meal is R79, four loaves are R64, and two packs of chicken are R178.

- The sample one-shop total is R1,016.
- Four weeks is R4,064.
- For three people that is about R338.67 each per week.

## A practical routine

- Update prices when a staple jumps, not every single trip.
- Keep cleaning on the list so it is not a surprise.
- If you shop twice a week, do not also multiply by four and by two.
