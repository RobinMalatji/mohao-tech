# Local business captions

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## This week only

Use when: You have a dated offer.

Write three captions for the offer I paste. Include the date and the price I gave you. Do not add a discount.

> Check: Price and date match my paste.

## Sold out

Use when: The item is gone.

Write a caption that says it is sold out and names when it returns only if I told you. Do not tease a restock.

> Check: No invented return date.

## Hours

Use when: The hours changed.

Write one caption with the new hours and the old hours so people are not surprised. Use only the hours I paste.

> Check: Both sets of hours are mine.

## Behind the counter

Use when: You want a process post.

Write a caption about the step I describe. Do not claim a certification or an award.

> Check: No award was added.

## Quiet day

Use when: You need a post and nothing is on sale.

Write a caption that tells people what is available today. Do not invent urgency.

> Check: There is no fake scarcity.

## Reply to a comment

Use when: A customer asked a question in public.

Draft a reply that answers only what I know. If I do not know, the reply says we will check.

> Check: The reply does not guess.
