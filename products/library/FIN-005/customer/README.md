# Paycheque split planner

*FIN-005 · version 1.0.0*

Divide one net pay across bills, savings, spending, and giving, and check that the split is 100%.

Enter net pay and the percentage you want in each job. The workbook converts percentages to rand and warns you when the percentages do not add up.
