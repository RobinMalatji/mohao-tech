# Paycheque split planner

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

A split is a rule you set before the month starts. The sample sends 55% of R28,500 to bills, 20% to savings, 20% to spending, and 5% to giving. Change the shares. The check row must equal 100%.

## How to use the workbook

1. Put net pay in C2.
2. Edit the four shares in column B. They are percentages, so 20% is 0.2.
3. Look at row 7. If the share check is not 1, the plan is incomplete.
4. Transfer the rand amounts the morning after payday, starting with bills and savings.

## Worked example

Bills are R15,675 in the sample.

- Savings and spending are R5,700 each.
- Giving is R1,425.
- The four shares add to 100% and the rand adds back to R28,500.

## A practical routine

- If a debit order is larger than the bills amount, fix the share before payday.
- Do not use the spending amount to cover a bill you forgot.
- Rewrite the split when your net pay changes.
