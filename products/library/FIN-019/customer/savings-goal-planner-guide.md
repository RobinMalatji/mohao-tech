# Savings goal planner

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

The laptop needs R11,200 more over 16 months, which is R700 a month. Moving costs need R1,500 a month. The course needs R400 a month. Together that is R2,600 from payday, and R33,200 is still unfunded.

## How to use the workbook

1. Enter the full target, what you have already saved, and the months you are willing to take.
2. Do not set months to zero. The formula returns zero so it does not divide by zero, and that is a signal to pick a date.
3. Read Monthly goals before you open another goal.
4. If payday cannot carry the total, extend a month count or pause a goal. Do not leave all three pretending they will happen.

## Worked example

Laptop monthly is R700.

- Moving monthly is R1,500.
- Course monthly is R400.
- The unfunded gap is R33,200.

## A practical routine

- Transfer the monthly goals on payday into separate envelopes or accounts.
- When you buy the item, set the target equal to saved so the monthly amount becomes zero.
- Do not include the emergency fund here. That has its own planner.
