# Product listing copy sheet

*This is a working draft for a South African online shop. It is not legal advice. Have a lawyer read it before you publish it as your policy.*

## One listing

Write the product name as the buyer searches for it. Then one sentence on the result. Then the material or format, the price, and the care or the file type.

- Name.
- Result.
- Format or material.
- Price.
- The question you are tired of answering.

## Sample

Name: Field notes for foremen. Result: a pocket book for site instructions. Format: 64 pages, soft cover. Price: R220. Question: it is not waterproof.
