# Job description prompts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## From the work

Use when: You have a list of tasks.

Turn this task list into a job description. Keep the tasks. Delete adjectives that do not change who would apply. Write the location and days on site only if I provided them.

> Check: No invented salary or perk.

## Must versus later

Use when: The list is too long.

Split the requirements into 'needed in the first month' and 'can be learned here'. Do not add requirements.

> Check: Every line came from my list.

## Inclusive pass

Use when: Before you post.

Point at lines that demand a degree, a tool, or a year-count that the tasks do not require. Do not rewrite the salary.

> Check: You decide which lines to cut.

## Application ask

Use when: You want useful applications.

Write the application instructions: three relevant projects, no photo, no identity number. Keep it under 120 words.

> Check: The instructions do not ask for sensitive data.

## Rejection note

Use when: After a decision.

Draft a short rejection that does not invent feedback. Thank them and state that we will not move forward.

> Check: No fake praise of a specific skill.

## Scorecard

Use when: Before interviews.

Turn the must-have tasks into six interview questions with what a good answer includes. Do not ask about family, health, or age.

> Check: Questions stay on the work.
