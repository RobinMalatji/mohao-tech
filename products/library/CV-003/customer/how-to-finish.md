# How to finish the Career-change CV kit

A CV that explains the move without apologising for the old career.

## Replace the sample

1. Put your name and city in the heading. A full street address is unnecessary.
2. Replace each role with yours. Keep the shape: action and result.
3. Delete a skill you cannot discuss for five minutes.
4. Translate the old work into the new job's tasks.
5. Put the target role in the heading, not a vague 'seeking opportunities'.

## Before you send

- The file name is YourName-Role.pdf.
- A person who does not know you can see the target role in the first three lines.
- You can defend every number in an interview.

> The employment history in this file is fictional sample text. Replace every line before you send it. This kit does not guarantee interviews.
