# Research briefing prompts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## Source list

Use when: You pasted notes.

List the sources I actually pasted. Do not add famous studies from memory.

> Check: Every source is in the paste.

## Claims

Use when: You need the findings.

List claims and the source for each. If two sources disagree, show both.

> Check: Disagreements stay.

## Not established

Use when: You are about to recommend.

Write what these sources do not establish.

> Check: The section is specific.

## Brief

Use when: You need one page.

Write a brief: question, what the sources say, what they do not say, what I might do next. Do not add statistics.

> Check: No new numbers.

## Quote check

Use when: You want a quotation.

Use a quotation only if it appears in my paste. Otherwise paraphrase and say it is a paraphrase.

> Check: Quotes are exact.

## Audience cut

Use when: A specific person will read it.

Cut the brief for that reader. Remove background they do not need. Do not remove the disagreements.

> Check: Disagreements remain.
