# Clearer business email worksheet

*This is a self-study pack: lessons, slides, and exercises. It is not an accredited qualification and it does not include a tutor.*

## One ask

Rewrite yesterday's email so the ask is the last line.

## The date

Add a date to an open request.

## No pile

Split a piled email into the one you will send.

## The subject

Rewrite five subjects from your sent mail.
