# Vehicle finance comparison

*FIN-007 · version 1.0.0*

Compare two vehicle offers using price, deposit, rate, and term.

Each offer becomes a monthly repayment estimate with the PMT formula. The workbook also shows the repayments over the full term and the extra you would pay above the amount financed. Balloon payments are not included.
