# Product FAQ builder

*This is a working draft for a South African online shop. It is not legal advice. Have a lawyer read it before you publish it as your policy.*

## The questions

What is the file? Which software opens it? Is it a Canva link? May I use it for clients? May I resell it? How do I download it again? What is the refund rule? Who do I write to?

- Answer from the product you actually made.
- If the answer is no, say no.
