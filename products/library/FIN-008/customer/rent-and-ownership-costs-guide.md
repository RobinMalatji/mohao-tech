# Rent and ownership cost comparison

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

This is a one-year cash comparison. It ignores transfer duty, bond registration, estate agent commission, and any change in the property's price. Those can dominate a short stay. The sample rent is R9,500 and the quoted repayment is R8,200, but levies and maintenance pull ownership above rent.

## How to use the workbook

1. Use the repayment from a quote, not a number you hope for.
2. Enter monthly levies, rates, insurance, and a maintenance reserve.
3. Read Ownership minus next rent. A positive number means the ownership year costs more than the next rental year.
4. Decide separately how long you expect to stay. This sheet cannot answer that.

## Worked example

Annual rent before the increase is R114,000.

- After an 8% increase it is R123,120.
- Ownership in the sample is R11,850 a month, or R142,200 a year.
- The gap is R19,080 for the year.

## A practical routine

- Ask for the levy statement and the rates account, not only the selling price.
- Keep a maintenance reserve even in a new building.
- Recalculate when the quote expires.
