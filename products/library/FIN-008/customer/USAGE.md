# How to use Rent and ownership cost comparison

*Excel workbook with PDF guide*

The sheet does not guess a bond. You enter the repayment a lender actually quoted, plus levies, rates, insurance, and maintenance. It then compares that year with rent and rent increases.

## Who it is for

Renters who have a written bond quote and want a one-year cost comparison.

## Compatibility

- Microsoft Excel for Microsoft 365 or Excel 2021 and later
- Formulas also open in Google Sheets; confirm PMT, FV, and NPER after import
- PDF guide opens in any PDF reader
- DOCX guide opens in Microsoft Word or Google Docs

## What you should see

- Use a quoted repayment instead of an invented bond
- Add levies, rates, and maintenance
- See the gap for one year, not a lifetime prediction
