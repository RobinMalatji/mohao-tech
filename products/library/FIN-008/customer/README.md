# Rent and ownership cost comparison

*FIN-008 · version 1.0.0*

Compare one year of rent with one year of ownership costs you type in.

The sheet does not guess a bond. You enter the repayment a lender actually quoted, plus levies, rates, insurance, and maintenance. It then compares that year with rent and rent increases.
