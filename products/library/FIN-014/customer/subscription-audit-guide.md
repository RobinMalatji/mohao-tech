# Subscription audit

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

The sample keeps music at R69 and cloud storage at R40. It marks video, gym, and news for cancellation. Those three cost R438 a month, or R5,256 a year, but only after the cancellation is confirmed.

## How to use the workbook

1. Export a month of bank transactions and add every repeating charge.
2. Use Keep or Cancel exactly.
3. Cancel with the provider, then change the monthly amount to zero only when a later statement proves it stopped.
4. The annual saving stays on the sheet until then so you do not count it early.

## Worked example

Keep is R109 a month.

- Cancel is R438 a month.
- The gym alone is R2,988 a year in the sample.

## A practical routine

- Do this twice a year.
- Shared family plans need one owner. Write that name in the note.
- Free trials belong on the list on the day they start.
