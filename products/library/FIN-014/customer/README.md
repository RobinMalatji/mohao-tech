# Subscription audit

*FIN-014 · version 1.0.0*

List recurring charges, mark what you will cancel, and total the annual saving.

Every row is a subscription or membership. Decision must be Keep or Cancel. Annual cost is monthly times 12. The summary adds the cancel rows.
