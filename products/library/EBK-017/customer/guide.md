# Backup habits for a small business

*Keep a copy of the files and the records that would hurt to lose.*

## What to copy

Customer files, invoices, the site, and the password manager export process your tool recommends. A laptop is not a backup. A folder on the same laptop is not a backup.

- Invoices.
- Client files.
- The site or the product files.

## Where

One copy in a cloud drive and one copy on a drive that is not always plugged in. Test that you can open a file from the copy. An untested backup is a rumour.

- Two places.
- One of them offline.
- Open a file from the copy.

## How often

Invoices and client work: weekly. A product you are editing: daily while you edit. Put the reminder on the calendar you already use.

- Weekly for records.
- Daily while editing.
- A calendar reminder.

## If the laptop dies

Write, on paper or in the password manager, how to reach the backup and the domain registrar. If only you know, the business stops when you are unavailable.

- Who else can get in.
- Where the backup is.
- The registrar and the host.
