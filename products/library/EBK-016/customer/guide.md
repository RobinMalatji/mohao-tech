# Hire a first contractor

*Brief, pay, and review a contractor without pretending they are staff.*

## The brief

Write the outcome, the files they will receive, the date, and how you will judge the work. A brief that says 'make it pop' is a dispute waiting.

- Outcome.
- Files and access.
- Date and the definition of done.

## The trial

Start with a paid piece of work that is real but small. An unpaid test is a bad signal and a bad sample. Pay the trial even if you do not continue.

- Paid.
- Small.
- Real work.

## Access

Give access to the files they need, not to every account you own. Remove access when the job ends. Do not share a password by chat if you can avoid it.

- Least access.
- Remove it after.
- No shared password in chat.

## The review

When the work arrives, compare it to the brief. Ask for the change that the brief supports. A new idea is a new brief, not a free revision.

- Compare to the brief.
- One revision if you agreed one.
- New ideas are new work.
