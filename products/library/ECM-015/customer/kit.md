# Marketplace listing notes

*This is a working draft for a South African online shop. It is not legal advice. Have a lawyer read it before you publish it as your policy.*

## What you still have to read

Each marketplace has its own category rules, fees, and image sizes. This kit is a preparation list: barcode or identifier, stock, who pays returns, and the image crop. It is not Takealot's official template and it does not include their fees.
