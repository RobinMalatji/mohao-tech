# Net worth snapshot

*FIN-013 · version 1.0.0*

List what you own and what you owe, then subtract.

Assets are split into cash you can reach and other assets. Liabilities are listed one by one. Net worth is assets minus liabilities. The workbook does not value a business or a property for you.
