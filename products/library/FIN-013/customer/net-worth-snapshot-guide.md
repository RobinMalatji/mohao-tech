# Net worth snapshot

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

The sample person has R14,500 in cash, a vehicle they value at R70,000, and R2,000 of furniture. Debts are R36,500. Net worth is R50,000. The vehicle value is the owner's estimate, not a dealer offer.

## How to use the workbook

1. Use cash balances from the banking app on the day you fill this in.
2. For a vehicle or furniture, use a conservative amount you could accept, and say so in the note.
3. Use statement balances for debts.
4. Read cash assets separately from net worth. A positive net worth can still be short of cash.

## Worked example

Cash is R14,500.

- Other assets are R72,000.
- Debts are R36,500.
- Net worth is R50,000.

## A practical routine

- Update this on the first of the month.
- Do not add the same asset twice because two people share it. Agree the share and enter only your share.
- A retirement fund can be added as its own row with kind Other if you know the latest statement value.
