# How to use Household monthly budget

*Excel workbook with PDF guide*

Plan one household month from net pay. The workbook totals income, totals each cost you list, and shows the surplus and two ratios: savings and housing.

## Who it is for

Households in South Africa who want a monthly plan in rand, including households with one main salary and a smaller second income.

## Compatibility

- Microsoft Excel for Microsoft 365 or Excel 2021 and later
- Formulas also open in Google Sheets; confirm PMT, FV, and NPER after import
- PDF guide opens in any PDF reader
- DOCX guide opens in Microsoft Word or Google Docs

## Before you start

1. Read the README and this usage guide.
2. Open the main deliverable and replace every sample figure or sample sentence with your own.
3. Keep a copy of the original download before you edit it.
4. Check the licence before you share the edited file with anyone else.

## What you should see

- See surplus after the costs you actually listed
- Check whether housing is taking more than you intended
- Keep a written note next to every figure
