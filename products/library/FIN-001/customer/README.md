# Household monthly budget

*FIN-001 · version 1.0.0*

A rand budget that separates take-home pay, committed costs, and what is left to save.

Plan one household month from net pay. The workbook totals income, totals each cost you list, and shows the surplus and two ratios: savings and housing.
