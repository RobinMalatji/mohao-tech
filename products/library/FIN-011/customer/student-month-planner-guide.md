# Student month planner

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

The sample student receives R2,500 from family, R3,000 as a living stipend, and R900 from a Saturday job. Residence, transport, data, and a textbook fund take R4,200. R2,200 is left, which is R550 a week.

## How to use the workbook

1. Enter only money you are sure will arrive.
2. Put residence and transport in Fixed before you decide on weekend spending.
3. Keep the textbook line even when you are not buying a book this month.
4. Use the weekly amount as the cash or card limit for food and personal costs.

## Worked example

Money in is R6,400.

- Fixed costs are R4,200.
- R550 a week is the sample limit after those costs.

## A practical routine

- If the Saturday shift is cancelled, set the wage to zero and read the week amount again.
- Do not use the textbook fund for a night out and promise to replace it later unless you actually transfer it back.
- Rewrite the plan when the stipend date moves.
