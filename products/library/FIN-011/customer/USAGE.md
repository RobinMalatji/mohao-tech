# How to use Student month planner

*Excel workbook with PDF guide*

The workbook totals money in, subtracts fixed campus costs, and divides what remains by four weeks. It is a spending plan, not a bursary application.

## Who it is for

University and college students managing a monthly allowance in rand.

## Compatibility

- Microsoft Excel for Microsoft 365 or Excel 2021 and later
- Formulas also open in Google Sheets; confirm PMT, FV, and NPER after import
- PDF guide opens in any PDF reader
- DOCX guide opens in Microsoft Word or Google Docs
