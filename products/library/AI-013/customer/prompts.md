# Sales follow-up prompts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## After the call

Use when: The call just ended.

Write a follow-up that lists the two points they cared about, from my notes. Ask one question. Do not add a new offer.

> Check: The points are from the notes.

## No reply

Use when: A week has passed.

Write a short note that refers to the proposal date and asks if it is still a priority. Do not invent urgency.

> Check: No fake deadline.

## They asked for a discount

Use when: You have not agreed.

Write a reply that says you will look at scope before price. Do not offer a number.

> Check: No discount was invented.

## Not now

Use when: They said next quarter.

Write a note that accepts the timing and asks what should be true next quarter. Do not push a meeting this week.

> Check: The timing is respected.

## Wrong person

Use when: They are not the buyer.

Write a note that asks who decides and offers a one-page summary they can forward.

> Check: You do not pressure them.

## Won

Use when: They said yes.

Write the confirmation of price, start date, and the next action I provide. Do not add terms.

> Check: Terms match what I pasted.
