# Abandoned cart email copy

*This is a working draft for a South African online shop. It is not legal advice. Have a lawyer read it before you publish it as your policy.*

## Email one, two hours later

Subject: Your files are still in the cart. Body: name the product, the price, and the link. No discount.

## Email two, the next day

Subject: A question about the cart. Body: ask if the format was unclear. Offer to answer. Still no discount unless you have a real, dated offer.
