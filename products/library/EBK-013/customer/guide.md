# Time blocking for operators

*Put the week's real work on a calendar before the week fills with chat.*

## Three kinds of time

Delivery, sales, and admin. If you do not block them, delivery eats the week and invoices go out late. Block them before you accept another meeting.

- One delivery block.
- One sales block.
- One admin block.

## How long

Start with ninety minutes. A four-hour fantasy block will be broken and then you will say the method failed. Protect the ninety minutes as a meeting with yourself.

- Ninety minutes.
- A name on the block.
- Notifications off.

## Meetings

Cluster meetings on two days if you can. A thirty-minute meeting in the middle of a delivery block costs the whole block. Say so when you decline.

- Cluster them.
- Decline one this week.
- Give a reason.

## Friday look

On Friday, look at the blocks you missed. Do not scold yourself. Move the work or drop it. A block you miss every week is a lie. Delete it.

- Which block was a lie.
- Move or delete.
- One change for next week.
