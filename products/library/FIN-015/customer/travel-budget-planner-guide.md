# Travel budget planner

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

Four nights at R1,600 a night is R6,400 for the room. Two people eating at R300 a day for five days is R3,000. Flights are R3,600 and one activity is R1,200. Ten percent contingency makes the sample trip R15,620, or R7,810 each.

## How to use the workbook

1. Enter the room rate, not a rate per person, unless you are booking beds separately. If you do, change the stay formula.
2. Food uses nights plus one so the travel days are included.
3. Set contingency before you book, and do not spend it on an upgrade.
4. Divide by travellers only if they are sharing these costs.

## Worked example

Stay is R6,400.

- Food is R3,000, not R2,400, because the sample counts five days.
- Contingency is R1,420.
- The trip total is R15,620.

## A practical routine

- When you book, replace the estimate with the receipt.
- If a person drops out, change Travellers and reread the per-person line.
- Keep contingency until you are home.
