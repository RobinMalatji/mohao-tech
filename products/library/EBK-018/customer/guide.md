# A brand voice page

*One page that tells a writer what you sound like, with examples.*

## Three lines

Write who you are talking to, what you will not claim, and one sentence that sounds like you. If you cannot read that sentence aloud, it is an advertisement, not a voice.

- The reader.
- Claims you refuse.
- One true sentence.

## Examples

Give a before and after. Before: 'We leverage cutting-edge solutions.' After: 'We build the booking page and the email that follows.' The after is the voice. Collect five of these from real work.

- Five befores and afters.
- Taken from real sentences.
- No word list of fifty adjectives.

## Words you avoid

List the words that make you sound like someone else. For many small firms that list includes 'passionate', 'world-class', and 'synergy'. Add the ones your customers do not say.

- A short avoid list.
- Words your customers do use.
- No thesaurus exercise.

## Use it

Put the page where the next email will be written. Review one email a month against it. A voice document nobody opens is decoration.

- Next to the writing.
- One email a month.
- Update an example when you find a better one.
