# Side-hustle profit calculator

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

Forty boxes at R120 is R4,800 of sales. Materials are R1,400, the 10% stall fee is R480, and delivery is R280. Profit is R2,640. Fourteen hours include baking and delivery, so the sample is R188.57 an hour before tax.

## How to use the workbook

1. Count hours from the moment you shop, not only the time at the table.
2. Enter the fee as a decimal. Ten percent is 0.1.
3. Include packaging in materials.
4. If profit per hour is below what you need, change the price or the hours and read the row again. The sheet will not tell you the 'right' price.

## Worked example

Sales are R4,800.

- Costs are R2,160.
- Profit is R2,640.
- The hourly figure is profit divided by 14.

## A practical routine

- Do this after every trading weekend while the receipts are still in the car.
- Set aside money for ingredients before you spend the profit.
- Tax on trading income is not calculated here.
