# Proposal drafting prompts

*These prompts draft text for you to edit. They do not check facts, quotes, or the law. Remove anything the model invented before you send it.*

## Scope

Use when: You have call notes.

Write the scope as included and excluded. Use only the notes. Put unknowns under questions.

> Check: Excluded is explicit.

## Price block

Use when: You have decided the price.

Write the price section using the number, currency, and what it includes. Do not add a discount.

> Check: The number is the one I typed.

## Timeline

Use when: You have a start and an end.

Write the timeline with those dates and the dependency I named. Do not promise a date I did not give.

> Check: Dates match.

## Assumptions

Use when: Things could change the price.

List assumptions from my notes. If an assumption is broken, say the price is revisited. Do not invent a penalty.

> Check: Assumptions come from the notes.

## Plain summary

Use when: The buyer is busy.

Write a 120-word summary a buyer can forward. No adjectives about our passion.

> Check: It can be forwarded as-is after you check it.

## Redline

Use when: They sent changes.

Compare their edits to my original scope and list what changed the work. Do not accept the change for me.

> Check: You decide.
