# Design a productised service

*Turn custom work into one offer with a fixed scope and a name.*

## Pick the repeated job

Look at the last ten projects. Find the slice you did every time. That slice is the candidate. The custom edges stay custom, or they stay out.

- Last ten projects.
- The repeated slice.
- The edges you will refuse.

## Name and boundary

Give it a plain name. Write what is included in a list a buyer can count. Write the time it takes you. If you cannot count the list, it is not productised yet.

- A name.
- A counted list.
- A duration.

## The first buyers

Offer it to three past clients before you build a brand around it. Their questions are the sales page. Do not hide it behind a waitlist.

- Three past clients.
- Write down their questions.
- Change the list, not the logo.

## Keep it still

Do not add a feature every time one buyer asks. Write the request down. Review the offer monthly. Stability is the product.

- A request list.
- A monthly review.
- No silent scope growth.
