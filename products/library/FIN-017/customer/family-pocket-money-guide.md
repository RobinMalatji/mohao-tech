# Family pocket-money planner

*Replace the sample figures before you rely on the result.*

The sample household pays R250, R120, and R80 a month. That is R450 a month and R5,400 a year. The notes say what each amount is for, so an increase has a reason.

## How to use the workbook

1. Add or remove a child by editing rows, then extend the SUM ranges if you add a fifth child.
2. Write what the money covers. If airtime is included, do not also refund airtime.
3. Read the annual total before you agree to a new amount.
4. Pay it on the same day each month.

## Worked example

Amina's year is R3,000.

- The household month is R450.
- The household year is R5,400.

## A practical routine

- Review in January and at the start of a new school year.
- If a grandparent also gives money, write that outside this total so you do not double count.
